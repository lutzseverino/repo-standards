# Finish authoring at a validated local standards source

`author-standards` discovers preferences and creates or revises the concrete
standards source, finishing after author review and validation. Repository
provisioning and publication use a separate workflow, and `adopt-standards`
applies published standards to an adopting project. This gives authors a useful
local deliverable without coupling preference discovery to hosting, release,
or adoption procedures, at the cost of a handoff before the source is adoptable.
